Questions

Before you order.

Which shape is best for a portrait?

Circle is usually the strongest choice for a single portrait because the radial thread geometry naturally frames the face. Square and rectangle work well for wider compositions.

What sizes can I request?

You can request small, medium, large or a completely custom size based on your wall and subject.

What photos work best?

Clear, well-lit images with a visible main subject and good contrast produce the strongest results.
